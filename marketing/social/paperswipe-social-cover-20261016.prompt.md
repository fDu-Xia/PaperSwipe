Create one finished premium HORIZONTAL social profile background for the Chinese research-paper discovery product PaperSwipe, 16:9 landscape, high resolution. This is final artwork, not a brand guideline board, not a mockup.
Image 1 is the existing PaperSwipe logo to incorporate faithfully: purple rounded-square app icon with the white folded P and delicate pink-lilac fold. Preserve that recognizable symbol and its proportions; remove the surrounding white margin when placing it, do not invent a different logo.
Brand positioning: a calm, approachable way for students and researchers to discover worthwhile academic papers by swiping cards, then save and plan reading.
Art direction: refined editorial graphic design meets sculptural paper illustration. A luminous warm ivory and very pale lavender backdrop with almost imperceptible fine paper grain, restrained rich violet accents, dark violet-charcoal typography. Quiet, confident, intellectual, tactile, beautiful rather than corporate.
Composition: generous quiet margins. Place an elegant moderately sized logo + PaperSwipe wordmark lockup near center-left, and beneath it the Chinese statement on ONE clean line: "找到值得读的论文。" Use a bold beautiful clean Chinese sans serif with impeccable spacing. No illegible pseudo-writing. Realistic paper thickness, subtle turned corner, beautifully soft studio shadows, tasteful depth. A single fine flowing lavender swipe arc subtly connects the paper sculpture to the composition. Avoid excessive decorative elements.
Keep ALL essential text and logo comfortably inside x=18%-77%, y=28%-57%. Keep the paper sculpture primarily x=63%-83%, y=28%-65%, not overlapping words. Not a website UI, no buttons, no phones, no QR code, no robot, no stock AI brain, no neon cyberpunk, no watermarks, no framing device. Opaque background.
